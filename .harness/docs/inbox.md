# Inbox

Lines written by hand or by a skill when something shows up that does not deserve an intent yet: a bug, a priority that changes, a template left behind in the repo where a skill was working. One line per entry, `- <YYYY-MM-DD>: <one line>`. A skill that notices something about this repo's harness writes here and carries on with what is there: it does not rerun `/harness-init`, it does not touch scripts, hooks, workflows or `AGENTS.md`, because the fix is made in the harness repo and comes back here by rerunning the phase that owns the file. `/board`, when it arrives, shows them at the opening and asks for each one whether it becomes an intent, a slice with `spec: inbox (<date>)`, or nothing. Until `/board` is there, they are read by hand when a session opens. Closed lines are removed, not ticked off: the history is in git.
