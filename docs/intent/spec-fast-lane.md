## Problem

`/spec` asks one question per message, and on a small intent most of them only confirm the default it already recommends.

## What success looks like

`/spec --fast <intent>` sends one message with the three or four questions whose answers change the spec, each with its recommended default; the spec it writes lists every default nobody was asked about under a heading of assumed decisions, and it lands like any other spec.

## Out of scope

A second skill or a new template. Changing the full interview, which stays the default. Deciding on its own when an intent deserves the fast lane.
